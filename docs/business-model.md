# Business Model: Independent Civil Labour Crew

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9312`
- ISCO-08: `9312`
- Occupation: Civil Engineering Labourers
- Social impact: local-jobs, worker-safety, infrastructure

## Customer

- small contractors
- municipal public-works crews
- site owners

## Offer

- site-prep task assignment
- material handling assist
- safety-brief and inspection checklist
- site-clearing sign-off
- reporting

## Revenue

- per-job fee
- monthly crew-operations platform fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no robot dispatch near excavations/heavy equipment without governor gate
- safety-critical zone entry requires human sign-off
- inspection checklists are auditable, not editable
